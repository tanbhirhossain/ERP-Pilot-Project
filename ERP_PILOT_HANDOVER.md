## 2. Architecture — Premium & Compliant

- **Modular Monolith:** `modules/{PARTNER,INVENTORY,ACCOUNTS,SALES}/` each with `Config/Database/Models/DTO/Interfaces/Repositories/Services/Requests/Http/Providers/Routes/Resources`
- **SOLID:** Single Responsibility (`StockService::move` only ledger writer), Open/Closed (providers via interface), Dependency Inversion (`InventoryStockProviderInterface` → SALES, `AccountsPostingProviderInterface` → SALES/POS)
- **Auto-discovery:** `App\Providers\ModuleServiceProvider` globs `modules/*/Providers/*ServiceProvider.php` — no manual registration
- **Vite Aliases:** `@partner/@inventory/@accounts/@sales` + `@core/@hrm` | **Menu:** `CORE/Config/menu.ts` 4 groups (Partner, Inventory, Accounts, Sales & POS)
- **CORE UI:** `DataTable` (BuildsDatatableQueries trait, server paginate/search/sort/capped 100), `SearchableSelect` (partner/inventory/accounts endpoints), `StatusBadge`, `MoneyInput`, `LineItemsRepeater`, `formatCurrency(BDT)`/`formatDate`
- **Security:** `PermissionMiddleware` on every route, 86 permissions (14 ACCOUNTS, 18 INVENTORY, 12 PARTNER, 10 SALES), Super Admin seeded, ActivityLog on Account/Journal/Quotation/Order/Invoice

## 3. Cross-Module DIP Integrations (No Tight Coupling)

```
SALES Order confirm  → INVENTORY InventoryStockProvider::reserveStock([product_id, warehouse_id, qty], SO-no)
SALES Invoice post   → INVENTORY InventoryStockProvider::deductStock([...], INV-no)  → stock_moves + weighted-avg stock
                     → INVENTORY InventoryValuationProvider::getUnitCost()           → COGS snapshot
                     → ACCOUNTS AccountsPostingProvider::postJournal(SV: AR 1100 Dr → Sales 4001 Cr + VAT 2110 + COGS/Inventory)
POS sell             → INVENTORY deduct + SALES Invoice → ACCOUNTS journal (same flow)
ACCOUNTS reports     → LedgerService::trialBalance/pl/bs reads acc_account_ledger (immutable)
```
